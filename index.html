Privacy Policy & Support

Privacy Policy

Effective Date: August 19, 2026

Metronome Companion is a native practice companion for musicians. Your practice information stays on your device.

Information Stored on Your Device

Metronome Companion stores information you enter into the app locally on your device so it can remember your practice history and preferences. This may include:

  • Practice piece titles
  • Composer names
  • Practice log dates
  • BPM values
  • Practice notes
  • App preferences, such as theme and screen-awake preference

Information We Do Not Collect

Metronome Comopanion does not collect or transmit your personal information or user-generated practice data to the developer or to an external server.

Metronome Companion does not use third-party analytics, advertising SDKs, tracking technologies, external crash-reporting services, external user accounts, or developer-operated cloud storage.

Data Storage and Backups

Your practice data and preferences are stored locally on your device. Metronome Companion does not upload this information to a developer-operated server, iCloud, or another cloud service.

Your app data may be included in Apple device or iCloud backups depending on your device and Apple account settings. These backups are handled by Apple and your device settings, not by Metronome Companion.

Deleting Your Data

You can delete your locally stored Metronome Companion data from within the app using the Clear All User Data option in Settings.

Deleting Metronome Companion from your device also removes its locally stored app data from that device, subject to any backups maintained separately by Apple according to your backup settings.

Changes to This Privacy Policy

This Privacy Policy may be updated from time to time. If changes are made, the updated policy will be posted on this page with a revised effective date.

About Metronome Companion

Metronome Companion is a practice companion for musicians that combines metronome tools with practice tracking and logging.

Metronome Companion does not require an account. Practice history and preferences are stored locally on your device.
